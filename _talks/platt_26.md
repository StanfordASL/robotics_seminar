abstract: "oday, most robot learning models ignore the geometry of the environment, focusing instead on enlarging model and dataset size. Whereas this approach has been very successful in language and vision, geometry is more important in robotics than in those fields. How can we leverage geometric sructural priors in robot learning? In this talk, I will summarize our recent work on this topic and make predictions about the future."

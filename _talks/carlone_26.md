title: "A Foundation-First Perspective on Spatial AI: Geometry, Memory, and Agents"
abstract: "Recent advances in geometric foundation models and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are challenging decades of conventional wisdom in robot perception, opening new opportunities to rethink how robots perceive, understand, and remember the world. In this talk, I will first explore how the internal representations learned by geometric foundation models can provide a common foundation for a wide range of tasks, from SLAM and visual-inertial navigation to scene-change detection and robotic manipulation. I will then shift the focus from geometry to memory, asking what information robots should retain from their observations and experiences to support long-term autonomy. I will discuss recent advances in structured and hierarchical memory representations, examining their benefits and limitations compared to MLLM-based full-context approaches. Finally, I will share my perspective on the emergence, impact, and limitations of agentic AI in robotics."

title: "Contact: The Problem and the Path to Robotic Dexterity"
abstract: "Contact is at the heart of robotic manipulation, yet it is also a central source of difficulty. Contact introduces hybrid, nonsmooth dynamics, where contact mode transitions and uncertainty in geometry and friction make physical interactions difficult to plan, predict, and control. In this talk, I will discuss how understanding contact can help us turn this challenge into an opportunity. We will explore four perspectives: leveraging contact structure to guide dexterous manipulation, developing compliance for more adaptable and robust behaviors, achieving dexterity with simpler mechanisms and environment contacts, and using contact to actively reduce uncertainty. Our broader vision is that better reasoning and control of contact interactions can expand dexterous manipulation capabilities, improve generalization and scalability, make robot interaction safer and more robust, and enable dexterity with simpler mechanisms."

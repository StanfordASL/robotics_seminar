title: "Skydio: Autonomy for a Family of Flying Robots"
abstract: "Skydio’s autonomy enables flying robots to navigate complex environments, track subjects, and execute missions through high-level operator commands. This talk explores the full autonomy stack powering Skydio’s flying robots, spanning the cloud and the edge. The recently unveiled F10 Lightrunner, an aircraft with a distinctive asymmetric design, extends autonomy to long-range fixed-wing flight and robotic takeoff and landing from MegaDock. These capabilities enable a single operator to orchestrate multiple drones remotely through a web browser, bringing autonomous robotics to public safety and critical infrastructure."

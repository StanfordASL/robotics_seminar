title: "The Foundation Trilogy – Improving Proficiency for Generalizable Autonomy"
abstract: "The promise of large-scale robot learning is to enable robot capabilities that generalize across embodiments and tasks. Recent advances have shown remarkable progress with an ever-growing complexity of tasks, embodiments, and environments. In contrast to hard-coded industrial robotic work, large-scale robot policies are often still brittle. They do not offer the high throughput desired for commercially viable real-world deployment. To replicate the success in language modelling, most current commercial efforts focus on increasing training data and scaling pretraining. This talk discusses what comes next. With advances in world modelling on the one hand and mid- / post- training techniques on the other, generalizable learning-based robot models are enabled to carry out tasks at a performance level beyond what they have seen in their training data. The talk will present some of our work towards that goal. It will cover training and test-time steering techniques to unlock the next level of robot capabilities."
